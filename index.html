Academic Grounding

Peer-Reviewed Literature Foundations

WinSecure's risk models, attack path synthesis, and resilience ratings are grounded directly in top-tier security papers published across IEEE, USENIX, and ACM conferences.

IEEE TDSC 2012

Bayesian Attack Graph Risk Models

Poolsappasit, Dewri, & Ray: "Bayesian Attack Graphs for Dynamic Risk Management". Replaces isolated checklists with formal directed probabilistic acyclic graphs to compute multi-hop exploit reachability.

ACSAC 2016

ShieldFS Ransomware Resilience

Continella et al.: "ShieldFS: A Self-healing, Ransomware-aware Filesystem". Evaluates 6 core anti-extortion pillars: CFA shielding, Volume Shadow Copy immutability, BitLocker, and canary detection.

CISA & NIST 2023

CISA Zero Trust Device Model

NIST SP 800-207 & CISA: "Zero Trust Maturity Model Version 2.0 (Device Pillar)". Evaluates endpoint maturity across 4 progressive tiers: Traditional → Initial → Advanced → Optimal.

IEEE S&P 2019

HOLMES Flow Correlation

Milajerdi et al.: "HOLMES: Real-Time APT Detection through Flow Correlation". Employs kill-chain causality correlation linking initial network entry to LSASS extraction and destructive execution.

HARDWARE SPEC

Control-Flow Enforcement (CET)

Intel Corporation & Microsoft: "Control-flow Enforcement Technology Specification". Evaluates CPU User Shadow Stacks and Arbitrary Code Guard preventing ROP/JOP shellcode execution.

USENIX / ACM

LOLBins Systematic Analysis

Gao et al.: "Systematic Analysis of Living-off-the-Land Attack Vectors on Modern OS". Enforces Microsoft Recommended WDAC block rules on abused binaries (certutil, mshta, wmic) and PowerShell CLM.

Mathematical Model

Objective Risk Penalty Engine

Deterministic scoring calculation eliminating subjective heuristics. Every deduction is weighted by finding severity, detection confidence, and asset importance.

Deduction Formula Specification

Deduction = Severity_Weight × Confidence × Criticality
Security_Score = 100.0 − ∑ Deductions
Base penalty multipliers configured in the risk engine:
  • Critical Misconfiguration: −15.0 pts (immediate unauthenticated compromise)
  • High Severity Exposure: −8.0 pts (credential leakage / privilege escalation)
  • Medium Severity Gap: −3.0 pts (suboptimal defensive posture)
  • Low Severity / Warning: −1.0 pt (telemetry or logging enhancement)
